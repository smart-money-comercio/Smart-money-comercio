# Treasury financing and configured publisher coverage — rules 23

Configured original publishers now enter the news editor independently of the changing opportunity top ten. A verified AMD feed candidate retains its issuer identity, original-source priority and inspectable story decision even when AMD is absent from the current ranking universe. Special-alert monitoring, rankings and numerical scores retain their existing scope. The 180-candidate limit remains; newly built editions record publisher candidates omitted by that limit.

The Treasury refunding profile reads a matching original policy statement. It distinguishes the total securities offered, maturing privately held debt, expected new cash, tenor-specific offering sizes and announced auction dates. Every field carries its original sentence and an announced-plan qualification. Conflicting totals, unsupported ranges, ambiguous offerings and invalid auction dates are withheld. A headline alone cannot supply this profile.

Committee reports and minutes are related documents, not Treasury financing decisions. Same-publication-day documents are grouped behind the substantive official policy statement. Committee titles alone do not fill news slots merely because their name contains “Borrowing.” The decision log keeps their individual decisions and related original references.

Amazon Investor Relations is added through its official RSS feed, with at most two fresh original article reads inside the existing shared read budget. The reader binds the release body to its complete headline and original zoned RSS publication. Older releases remain dated coverage, not fresh news. Publisher access failures retain the existing cooldowns.

New Treasury evidence uses schema 9. Existing evidence profiles, saved report and narration hashes, schema 2 video exports, source references and the private review requirement remain supported. No credentials, runtime evidence, publication records, ranking files, paid narration or uploads are changed by the installer.
