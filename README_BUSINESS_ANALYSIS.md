# Business interpretation and narration, rules 31

New daily editions and Weekend Outlook builds record business interpretation version 1. The checks reconstruct that review from the admitted source evidence.

The comparison distinguishes new contract awards, development progress and maintenance designations. It uses the event stage and extracted fields. A development program's existing contract value stays program context. An award's stated value and term describe its disclosed commercial scope. Funded work, delivery costs and revenue timing remain separate analytical checks. The comparison applies to represented defense companies with distinct business stages.

Daily briefs integrate the interpretation into the story and defense passages. The decision log lists the business channel and supporting fields. Narration expands the program and accounting terms, removes repeated category transitions and starts with the substantive takeaway. The brief's numerical scores retain their original values.

Weekend Outlook retains its written return basis, five-day calendar, source references and observation clocks. Speech consolidates the weekly comparison dates, puts publication dates into the story attribution and uses specific conditional business checks. The native media contract stays schema 2 with narration revision 2. The scene sequence follows the same admitted sections and later-horizon notice rules.

Archived daily and weekend records keep their original wording, hashes and JSON exports. Old records omit the new business version. New builds use rules 31, and a cached rules-30 presentation is rebuilt through the normal source checks. Existing scheduled weekend preparation calls the same build and export APIs.
