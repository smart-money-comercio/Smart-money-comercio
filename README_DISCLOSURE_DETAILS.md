# Original disclosure details — editorial rules 27

Rules 27 adds two bounded original-body readers: aircraft maintenance-depot designations and targeted Treasury money-services sanctions. A complete original report, publisher identity, fresh observation and verified publication clock remain prerequisites. Headline wording selects the reader; it cannot provide the facts.

Maintenance evidence identifies the company-reported designation, aircraft platform, facility location and explicitly planned services. The designation is kept separate from funded orders, completed services, contract value and revenue. The business follow-up concerns service orders, site utilization, staffing and revenue timing. Ambiguous sites, another company's designation, negation, forecasts and historical wording do not establish a current designation.

Financial-sanctions evidence identifies the formal action, target group, location and an explicit digital-asset channel. The corresponding portfolio lens concerns payment and counterparty exposure. It requires company disclosures before asserting exposure or earnings effects. Genuine tariff and export-control stories retain their trade-policy interpretation.

Every extracted field retains its original sentence. `/storylog NOC` shows those sentences and business terms that the extracted facts do not establish. Incomplete material facts produce a held candidate rather than a headline-only selection. Source hashes, URLs, observation clocks, publication clocks, stage and fact values are revalidated exactly.

## Original-body and publication controls

The direct Northrop research reader now uses its identified article body rather than navigation and related stories from the whole page. New direct-discovery proofs mark that scope. An older unscoped discovery candidate is held for a scoped review; saved editions keep their original proof validation.

An explicit Northrop release dateline must agree with its publication metadata. A newer CMS date cannot replace a conflicting original dateline, and a dateline alone cannot invent a zoned timestamp. A conflicting index row loses its unverified date. Northrop's original-source cache profile advances from 3 to 4 so the next normal reader call reprocesses the cached material without resetting its observation clock merely because the software changed.

The live C-130 release could not be fetched during preparation because its publisher returned HTTP 403. Its actual original date and current accessibility remain unverified here. No date from a secondary site is substituted. The supplied production edition is not rewritten, and an older or conflicting article may be held in a new edition.

## Presentation and compatibility

New supported stories use factual summaries instead of reading their headlines. Rules-27 voiceovers pronounce C-130 as "C one thirty" and use the letter names for AMD where appropriate. New reader copy uses "AMD's business outlook" and refers to the next session after a completed session. These presentation changes have an explicit version gate. Saved rules 19–26 report/script hashes and schema-2 YouTube export requirements remain intact.

Premarket schedules, delivery controls, risk grades, research scoring and allocation weights are unchanged by this update. This is an evidence-reading and interpretation update, not a Weekend Outlook release or a validation of predictive performance.
